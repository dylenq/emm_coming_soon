Submission Instructions

Themes, accepted submission types, formatting, key dates, and full author guidelines for the Journal of Mental Health and Wellbeing.

Themes

The Journal of Mental Health and Wellbeing publishes scholarly and practice-based contributions under the following core themes:

  • Clinical and Counselling Psychology
  • Mindfulness and Contemplative Practices.
  • Buddhism and Mental Health
  • Mental Health Promotion and Wellbeing.
  • Applied and Positive Psychology
  • Integrative and Holistic Healing.
  • Psychology Education and Innovation in Teaching
  • Reflective and Opinion Articles

Types of Submissions Accepted

  • Research Articles
  • Review Papers
  • Case-Based Studies
  • Theoretical Papers
  • Reflective Essays and Opinion Pieces

Submission Guidelines

  • Word Count: 2,000–5,000 words
  • Abstract: 150–250 words
  • Keywords: 3–5
  • Referencing Style: APA 7th Edition
  • File Format: Microsoft Word or PDF
  • Ethical Statement: All submissions must include a declaration of originality, ethical compliance, and disclosure of conflicts of interest.

For detailed instructions, please review the Author Guidelines section below.

Key Dates

  • Submission Deadline: 20th December 2025
  • Expected Publication: 20th March 2026

Submissions should be sent via email to info@emm.lk / info@everymindmatters@gmail.com with the subject line: "Manuscript Submission – [Author Name]"

Join as a Reviewer or Editorial Contributor

We also welcome expressions of interest from qualified professionals who wish to serve as peer reviewers or editorial board members.

Please write to info@emm.lk / info@everymindmatters@gmail.com with your CV and a brief statement of interest.

Author Guidelines

The Journal of Mental Health and Wellbeing welcomes original contributions that advance understanding and practice in psychology, mindfulness, and mental health. Authors are requested to carefully review and adhere to the following submission guidelines to ensure a smooth review and publication process.

Manuscript Length

Articles should typically range between 2,000–5,000 words, including references, tables, and appendices. Shorter reflective or practice-based pieces may be accepted at the discretion of the editorial board.

Abstract and Keywords

  • Each manuscript must include an abstract of 150 - 250 words summarizing the main objectives, methods, findings, and conclusions of the paper.
  • Provide 3 - 5 keywords that best describe the subject matter of the article.

Formatting Requirements

  • File Format: Submissions should be in Microsoft Word (.docx) or PDF format.
  • Font: Times New Roman, 12-point font.
  • Spacing: 1.5 line spacing throughout.
  • Headings: Use clear, consistent heading levels.
  • Page numbers: Include in the footer.

Referencing Style

All references and in-text citations must follow the APA 7th Edition style. Authors are encouraged to ensure accuracy and completeness of all references cited in their manuscripts.

Copyright and Licensing

By submitting a manuscript, authors agree that their work may be published online under the Every Mind Matters publication umbrella.

Ethical Compliance

All submissions must be original and must not have been published or under review elsewhere.

Authors are required to include a Declaration of Originality and Ethical Compliance at the end of their manuscript, confirming that:

  • The work is their own and properly acknowledges all sources.
  • All ethical standards have been adhered to, particularly for studies involving human participants.
  • Any potential conflicts of interest are clearly stated.

Submission Process

Manuscripts should be submitted electronically via email to info@emm.lk with the subject line "Manuscript Submission – [Author Name]".

All submissions will undergo editorial review and, where appropriate, double-blind peer review.

Review and Publication

Authors will be notified of the review outcome within 4 - 6 weeks. Accepted manuscripts may be subject to minor revisions before final publication.

Contact

For inquiries related to submissions or formatting, please contact: info@emm.lk / info@everymindmatters@gmail.com
