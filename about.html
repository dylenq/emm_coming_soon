About Us

Our Philosophy at Every Mind Matters

Holistic, person-centred mental health care that nurtures the mind, body, and inner self — empowering individuals to lead more mindful, connected, and fulfilling lives.

At Every Mind Matters (EMM), we believe that mental health is a vital pillar of overall well-being. It shapes how individuals think, feel, and behave, directly influencing how they handle stress, build relationships, and make decisions throughout every stage of life — from early childhood to adulthood.

Our approach is firmly rooted in holistic mental health care. We recognise that true wellness cannot be achieved by treating symptoms in isolation. Instead, we focus on nurturing the mind, body, and inner self — acknowledging the interconnectedness of emotional, physical, social, and spiritual dimensions of health.

When one aspect of well-being is affected, it inevitably impacts the others. At EMM, we provide integrated, person-centred care that supports balance, resilience, and sustainable growth. Through this comprehensive model, we empower individuals to lead more mindful, connected, and fulfilling lives.

About Us

Who We Are

Every Mind Matters Pvt Limited (PV 00269950) is a Sri Lankan-based mental health and wellness organisation committed to providing holistic psychological care. The company offers a wide range of services including counselling and psychotherapy, corporate wellness programs, therapeutic mindfulness, and mental health education initiatives.

Guided by a client-centred and integrative approach, Every Mind Matters addresses the physical, emotional, spiritual, and social dimensions of well-being. The organisation aims to break the stigma around mental health while empowering individuals and communities to build resilience, improve emotional intelligence, and lead balanced lives.

Our History

How Every Mind Matters Began

2019

Humble Beginning

Initiated as a private individual counselling practice, stepping into the field of mental health with a commitment to support and empower individuals on their healing journeys.

2020

Building Awareness

Expanded presence through digital platforms, using social media to raise awareness, share knowledge, and reduce stigma around mental health in Sri Lanka.

2021/22

Community Outreach

Launched outreach programs aimed at educating the general public, conducting mental health awareness campaigns, and facilitating conversations to normalise mental well-being.

2023

Official Establishment

Registered as Every Mind Matters Pvt Limited under Sri Lankan regulatory authorities, marking a new chapter of structured growth and professional service delivery.

2024

Expanding Impact

Collaborated with corporate entities, educational institutions, and community organisations to deliver holistic mental health services — integrating psychological, emotional, social, and spiritual support across sectors.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To provide culturally competent and wellness-focused psychological services that promote individuals' social-emotional development, prevent mental health challenges, and address social-emotional problems that currently exist — using evidence-based strategies for the betterment of society.

Our Mission

To show people the path — to bring them closer to themselves. True healing begins with self-awareness. Through guidance, support, and empowerment, individuals can reconnect with their inner selves and live with greater clarity and purpose. We are committed to breaking the stigma around mental health and spreading the awareness that help is available — just ask for it.

How We Work

Our Approach

At Every Mind Matters, we recognise that mental health is influenced by a wide range of personal, social, and environmental factors. Addressing these complexities requires more than traditional psychotherapy alone — it demands a comprehensive, client-centred approach.

Our process begins by carefully identifying the key factors that positively impact each client's mental well-being. From there, we create tailored pathways to healing through our diverse range of services. As a multidisciplinary mental health and wellness provider, EMM offers integrated solutions that support individuals holistically — emotionally, psychologically, socially, and spiritually.

We are committed to removing the shame and fear often associated with mental health. By fostering a safe and stigma-free environment, we encourage individuals to seek help early — because early intervention is crucial. Research shows that timely diagnosis and appropriate care significantly improve recovery outcomes and long-term mental wellness. Each client's journey is guided with empathy, professionalism, and personalised care.
